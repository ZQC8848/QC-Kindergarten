# Selling Crutches

**Cast**: Haide, the con artist. Fufu, his partner. Qunzhu, a cook cycling past.

[A little stall at the roadside, a few pairs of crutches laid out on the ground. Haide sits behind it; Fufu is beside it, calling out to passers-by.]

**Fufu**: Crutches! Good solid crutches! … (turning round) A whole morning and not one pair sold.

**Haide**: Because you're calling to the wrong people. Who with two good legs listens to "crutches"?

**Fufu**: What about people with bad legs?

**Haide**: People with bad legs bought theirs ages ago. The business isn't with them.

**Fufu**: Then who's it with?

**Haide**: People with good legs.

**Fufu**: (eyes lighting up) GREAT idea! Let's bet on it. The next person who comes past, whoever it is, if you can sell them a pair—

**Haide**: Whoever it is?

**Fufu**: Whoever it is!

[A bicycle bell in the distance. Qunzhu rides up on an old bicycle, a big ladle hanging from the handlebars.]

**Fufu**: (whispering) Here he comes! Legs are fine, look at him pedal!

**Haide**: (narrowing his eyes) Stop.

**Qunzhu**: (braking hard, nearly falling off) Huh? Me? … Whoa! You're the husky who solves puzzles!

<!-- illustration:1|"Whoa! You're the husky who solves puzzles!" -->

**Haide**: Mm. Get off. Walk a few steps for me.

**Qunzhu**: What for?

**Haide**: When you got off just now, which foot touched down first?

**Qunzhu**: (after a long think) … Don't know.

**Haide**: I know. The left. And gently. Why would someone get off a bike and put their left foot down gently?

**Qunzhu**: … Why?

**Haide**: Because deep down he knows his left foot isn't right.

**Qunzhu**: Deep down I don't know that!

**Fufu**: (leaning in) Deep down you do. Your brain just hasn't caught up!

[Qunzhu looks down at his legs. Haide leans in and sniffs his knee.]

**Haide**: Mm.

**Qunzhu**: Mm… meaning what?

**Haide**: These legs are new, aren't they?

**Qunzhu**: (startled) How did you know? I used to be a shark. I only grew legs the other week!

**Haide**: New legs. Not broken in yet. I've got four legs; I know more about legs than you do. Right, lift your left foot.

[Qunzhu stands on one leg, wobbling.]

**Haide**: Wobbly?

**Qunzhu**: Wobbly.

**Haide**: Other foot.

[Qunzhu switches to his right. It wobbles too.]

**Qunzhu**: That one's wobbly too!

**Haide**: Both wobbly. Means the trouble's already gone from the left side to the right.

**Fufu**: That was quick!

**Qunzhu**: (voice cracking) What do I do?

**Haide**: Squat.

[Qunzhu squats. His knee goes *click*. Silence.]

**Fufu**: (gasping) … Did you hear that?

<!-- illustration:2|His knee goes click -->

**Qunzhu**: I, I heard it…

**Haide**: (sighing, shaking his head) I wasn't going to say.

**Qunzhu**: Say it! I'm a person now, I can take it!

**Haide**: Ride that bike three more days and that leg won't be yours any more.

[Qunzhu bursts into tears.]

**Fufu**: (patting him) Don't cry, don't cry, there's a way! Isn't there?

**Haide**: There's a way. Legs can't stand strain. From today you don't let it strain at all. You get something else to do the straining for it—

**Qunzhu**: Something like what?

[Haide says nothing. With one paw he pushes a pair of crutches towards him.]

**Qunzhu**: … Crutches?

**Haide**: The sooner you start, the sooner you're better.

**Fufu**: (lowering her voice) Start late, and it's crutches for life.

**Qunzhu**: (grabbing them) How much?

**Haide**: How much have you got?

**Qunzhu**: (going through every pocket and coming out with only a ladle) … I'm a cook on my way to work. All I brought was a ladle.

**Haide**: (glancing at the bicycle) Forget it, then.

**Qunzhu**: Don't forget it! (pushing the bicycle over) This! Have this! I can't ride it any more anyway!

**Haide**: (hesitating) … Fine. Only because I feel sorry for you.

[Qunzhu gets up on the crutches, limps two steps, then turns round, eyes brimming.]

**Qunzhu**: Thank you! If it weren't for you, I'd have lost this leg!

**Haide**: (a wave of the paw) Off you go. Remember: no strain.

[Qunzhu limps off into the distance. Fufu watches him go, then looks at Haide.]

**Fufu**: … He's limping worse going than he was coming.

**Haide**: Once the mind limps, the leg follows.

**Fufu**: So I lost. What did we bet, anyway?

**Haide**: Forgot. (sniffs the bicycle and wrinkles his nose) Do you want this bike? I drive a Ferrari.

[Curtain]
