# Luyao and the Zha

A round golden moon hangs in a deep blue sky, and beneath it lies the sandy ground by the sea, planted as far as the eye can see with emerald-green watermelons. In the middle of it stands a boy in a black peaked cap with a silver badge and a long double-breasted greatcoat, a steel pitchfork in his hand, thrusting with all his might at a little orange-furred zha that is hugging a slice of watermelon and munching away happily. Out in the middle of the melon patch, a second little zha with golden fur stares with perfectly round eyes and reaches out to save her, too late. But the orange zha only gives her body a twist, and escapes between his legs.
