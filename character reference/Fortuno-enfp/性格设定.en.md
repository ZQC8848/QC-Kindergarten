# Fortuno (ENFP) character bible

> One line: The luckiest kid in the kindergarten. The coin always comes up heads, he always draws first, and the rain stops when he walks outside. The one thing he can't do is keep a plant alive.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Fortuno |
| MBTI | ENFP (Campaigner / little-lucky-charm type) |
| Look | A round mop of springy chestnut curls, a big grin with one front tooth missing, a lemon-yellow toggle coat, yellow rain boots, a four-leaf clover on his chest |
| Colours | Lemon yellow × cream × chestnut |
| Signature props | A coin that always lands heads, a chipped lemon-yellow toy watering can, a four-leaf clover pin |

## Core personality

**E (Extraverted)**: Can't sit still for a second. Whatever he sees, he wants to drag someone over to see it too.

**N (Intuitive)**: One idea after another, and the next is always more fun than the last.

**F (Feeling)**: Holds nothing back from the people he likes. Whoever he likes, the whole kindergarten knows by the next day.

**P (Perceiving)**: Never plans, because he's never needed to: things always turn out well. He has yet to see an exception.

## Personality in the design

- **The round mop of chestnut curls**: comb it however you like, it springs back. Like him, it won't stay down.
- **The grin with a front tooth missing**: he never holds a laugh back, so the whole kindergarten has seen the gap.
- **The lemon-yellow coat**: it has never been rained on. The rain stops when he walks outside, but he wears it every day anyway, because it looks good.
- **The yellow rain boots**: covered in mud. The rain may have stopped, but the puddles haven't, and he has to stamp in every one.
- **The four-leaf clover on his chest**: the first one he ever found.
- **Clear-sky blue eyes**: the weather follows him around, so his eyes are clear too.

## Habits

- Decides everything with a coin flip. The coin has never once come up tails.
- At nap time he hangs upside down off the top bunk to talk to Whiskle on the bottom one, until Whiskle pulls the blanket over his head.
- Walks with his head down, because there's always something on the ground waiting for him: someone's lost eraser, a four-leaf clover, a sweet.
- Goes round with his watering can looking for plants to water. Everyone is trying to stop him.
- Catchphrases: "Whiskle, look!" "Come on, together!" "It's fine, I'm lucky."

## Expressions and moods

The truest faces on his sheet are **the big laugh** and **the delighted surprise**. Anger arrives fast and leaves faster. There's only one kind of sad: another plant has wilted in his hands. He crouches in front of the pot for a long time, the only time anyone has seen him go quiet. Embarrassed, he scratches his curls, which only makes them worse.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: drawing lots, scratch cards, finding things, puddles after rain, being around Whiskle.
- Dislikes: waiting. Waiting for lunch, waiting in line, waiting for a seed to sprout.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: optimistic, warm, generous; everything good he finds, he gives away. With him around, bad things have a way of turning good.
- **Growth area**: luck means he never learned to wait. Anything that takes time, he's bad at; anyone who takes time, he gets too close to, too fast.
- **Direction**: sit in front of one plant for a week and do nothing at all.

## Relationships

- In the nap room, **Whiskle** sleeps in the bunk below his. Whiskle is who he most wants to be around in the whole kindergarten, and the only one who always seems to be leaning away. He doesn't understand why: he's loud, he asks Whiskle's plants "why aren't you growing?" every day, and any plant he touches droops. Whiskle can't stand any of the three, but all Fortuno remembers is that he was helping. On the very first day he got it right that Whiskle is a boy. Asked how he could tell, he said he guessed.
- Most of **Fufu**'s "super great ideas" go wrong. With Fortuno around, every bad idea works. They are the most dangerous pair in the kindergarten; **Luyao** gets a headache the moment he sees them standing together.
- **Haide** is the smartest in the group; Fortuno is the luckiest. Any game that comes down to chance, Haide cannot beat him. Brains losing to luck is the one thing Haide refuses to accept.
- **Dianer** says, "Why does that happen? Hang on, let me work it out." He wrote down the results of a hundred of Fortuno's coin flips, one by one, and the numbers don't hold up. The page has one word on it: "Impossible."

## Extra: The kindergarten's lucky charm, who can't keep one plant alive

- **His luck isn't a little better than everyone else's.** A hundred coin flips, a hundred heads. He always draws first. The rain stops when he walks outside. Whatever he feels like eating, that's what the snack is that day.
- **Plants are the one exception.** Every plant he has watered has died. Not too much water, not too little. They just die. Dianer can't work out why. Whiskle says maybe they can feel you rushing them.
- **Luck can decide what you run into. It can't make something grow.** Everything he has ever got arrived all at once, so he has no idea how to wait.
- **The day they drew lots for nap-room beds, he got the top bunk.** The bottom bunk was Whiskle. He says it's the best draw he's ever had. That week, Whiskle moved his flowerpots twice.
- **He has found thirty-seven four-leaf clovers.** The first is pinned to his chest; he has tried to give all the others away. Only one person refused. Whiskle said: "I'll grow my own."

## Meaning of the props

**The coin** is his whole outlook: it'll come up heads anyway. **The watering can** is the thing he most wants to get right and never once has: he wants to help Whiskle water the plants. **The four-leaf clover** is the first he ever found; as far as he's concerned, that's the day his luck began.
