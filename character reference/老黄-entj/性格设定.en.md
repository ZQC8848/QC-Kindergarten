# Laohuang (ENTJ) character bible

> One line: The youngest kid in the kindergarten, and everyone calls him Laohuang, "Old Huang". He calls every boy "little brother" and every girl "little sister". He's never in a hurry and never loses his cool, because he drinks cooling tea every day.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Laohuang |
| MBTI | ENTJ-A (Commander / little-boss type) |
| Look | Short dark-brown hair, a sure little smile, a white shirt under an old-tea yellow sweater vest, a ring of keys at his belt, legs crossed |
| Colours | Old-tea yellow × white × dark brown |
| Signature props | A white porcelain gaiwan (one jasmine flower floating in it), a can of Wong Lo Kat, the ring of keys |

## Core personality

**E (Extraverted)**: Knows everyone, remembers what each of them is good at, and what each of them owes him.

**N (Intuitive)**: Plays the long game. Everyone else is thinking about this afternoon; he's thinking about next term.

**T (Thinking)**: Business first, feelings after.

**J (Judging)**: His plans never change, because he got them right the first time.

**A (Assertive)**: Never doubts himself. When something goes wrong: "That one's on me. Next time it won't be." And next time it isn't.

## Personality in the design

- **The yellow sweater vest over a white shirt**: a kindergartner dressed for a meeting.
- **The ring of keys at his belt**: he has a key to nearly every door in the kindergarten, and nobody remembers who gave them to him, or when. One of them opens a locker stacked full of Wong Lo Kat.
- **Legs crossed**: the moment he sits down. As far as he's concerned, the matter is already settled.
- **The white gaiwan**: in Fuzhou, business starts over tea. His does too. It's just that what goes into the gaiwan is Wong Lo Kat.
- **The sure little smile**: before you've opened your mouth, he knows what you're going to say.

## Habits

- Every morning he sits at the little stone table under the big banyan by the gate. That's his office.
- Before any decision, he warms the bowl, cracks open a can of Wong Lo Kat with a *pssht*, pours it into the gaiwan, and floats one jasmine flower on top. Cooling tea is still tea.
- Calls every boy "little brother" and every girl "little sister", the way old men do in Fuzhou. He is the youngest one here.
- When someone gets worked up, he slides a can across to them.
- Never runs; stands with his hands behind his back. Wakes from nap time exactly on the minute.
- Catchphrases: "Little brother, sit. Tea first." "Keep your cool. Have a can." "Wander seven ways or eight, you still end up at Laohuang's."

## Expressions and moods

The truest face on his sheet is **that sure little smile**. He hardly has big expressions: surprise is one raised eyebrow, happiness is the smile going a little deeper. Nobody here has ever seen him angry. He puts it down to the cooling tea; as far as he's concerned, staying calm is down to the tea, not his temperament. When he's embarrassed he looks down and takes a sip, as if savouring it.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: Wong Lo Kat, jasmine flowers, the stone table under the banyan, a plan going off without a hitch, people coming to him to get things done.
- Dislikes: last-minute changes, being interrupted while he's talking business, running out of Wong Lo Kat.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: steady, far-sighted, reliable. Whatever you hand him gets done.
- **Growth area**: he trusts his plans so much that anything he can't fit into them, he treats as not there. Faced with someone's feelings, his first move is to hand over a can, not to ask what's wrong.
- **Direction**: ask "What's wrong?" before reaching for the can.

## Relationships

- He and **Luyao** are both ENTJs: one shouts, the other arranges. Every time Luyao blows, Laohuang slides a can of Wong Lo Kat across the stone table without a word. Luyao has never drunk one, out of pride; he has never thrown one away either, and there's a row of them in his drawer by now. Being called "little brother" by the youngest kid in the place is something Luyao still hasn't got used to.
- **Mushi** is the only one who knows Laohuang's full name, and it is the only secret Mushi, who can't keep secrets, has ever kept. What the terms were, neither of them has said. All anyone knows is that Mushi had a bowl of "tea" under the banyan that day.
- Laohuang organised the draw for nap-room beds, and **Fortuno** drew the top bunk. Fortuno is the one variable Laohuang's plans can't account for: however he sets things up, Fortuno comes first.
- **Haide** likes to make trouble. Laohuang doesn't fight him; he gives him a job. A troublemaker with something to do stops making trouble: that's Laohuang's theory. Whether Haide ever actually does the job is another matter.

## Extra: An old man from Fuzhou, living in the youngest kid in the kindergarten

- **"Laohuang" was his own idea.** On his first day he shook everyone's hand and said, "Just call me Laohuang." Nobody has called him anything else since. Only Mushi knows his full name.
- **"Little brother" and "little sister" are how Fuzhou does it.** In Fuzhou the older generation calls young people that, and he has done it since his first day here, Luyao included.
- **He has a plan.** His favourite line is "I have a plan." But he's from Fuzhou, where f and h blur together: in Chinese "a plan" is *fang'an*, and in his mouth it comes out *huang'an*, "a ridiculous plan". Everyone thinks his plans sound ridiculous. Every one of them works.
- **"Wander seven ways or eight, you still end up at Laohuang's."** He adapted it from an old Fuzhou saying: wander seven ways or eight, you never leave Fuzhou. However a matter winds around, it ends up on his table. So far, he's been right.
- **Cooling tea is still tea.** He looks as if he's doing a proper tea ceremony: warming the bowl, pouring, breathing in the aroma, every step. What goes in is Wong Lo Kat. The whole kindergarten knows. Nobody says so.

## Meaning of the props

**The gaiwan** is his style: things should be settled sitting down, slowly. **The Wong Lo Kat** is his calm, and the way out he offers everyone else: keep your cool, have a can. **The ring of keys** is his territory: in this kindergarten, there's no door he can't open.
