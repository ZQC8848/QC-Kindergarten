# Jiuyue (ISFP) character bible

> One line: She looks like she's daydreaming. She has seen everything; she just doesn't say. What she sees, she paints on fallen leaves and leaves where they lay.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Jiuyue |
| MBTI | ISFP (Adventurer / little-painter type) |
| Look | A low ink-black bun with a teal sheen, a paintbrush stuck through it, one thin vermilion cord, a white smock splashed with teal paint |
| Colours | Autumn-water teal × White Dew silver × a trace of vermilion |
| Signature props | A ginkgo leaf with a tiny painting on it, a jar of brush water, the paintbrush in her bun |

## Core personality

**I (Introverted)**: Happiest alone. Not afraid of people; she just thinks most things don't need saying.

**S (Sensing)**: Lives in what's in front of her. Colour, light, a reflection on water, the veins of one leaf. What she notices is specific, so specific nobody else saw it.

**F (Feeling)**: Her likes and dislikes run strong. They aren't written on her face. They're written in her paintings.

**P (Perceiving)**: Paints when she wants, leaves when she wants. No plan, and no need for anyone to understand why she has suddenly crouched down to stare at a puddle for ten minutes.

## Personality in the design

- **Half-lidded eyes and a small closed mouth**: her default face reads as daydreaming. She's looking. She's looking harder than anyone.
- **A loose low bun with a paintbrush stuck through it**: she doesn't care how her hair looks. She cares that the brush is always within reach.
- **That thin vermilion cord**: the only red on her, head to toe. Nobody has asked where it came from. She hasn't said.
- **The smock and fingertips stained teal**: she never washes them clean. The paint is her diary.
- **Autumn-water teal eyes**: they seem to be looking somewhere far away. They're looking at you.

## Habits

- Stops without warning, crouches, and looks at a leaf, a puddle, a strip of light for a long time.
- Paints very small pictures on fallen leaves she picks up. She doesn't keep them. She leaves them where someone will pass: a windowsill, the shoe rack, under the slide.
- Talks very little, and very briefly. If one word will do, she will not use two.
- If you ask "what are you painting?", she turns the picture away.
- Catchphrases: "Mm." "…Pretty." "Not showing you."

## Expressions and moods

The truest faces on her sheet are **the daydream** and **the faint smile**. A real smile is rare; people who have seen one remember it for a long time. Angry, she doesn't argue. She turns and walks off, and never looks your way again. Sad, she paints a great many paintings. Embarrassed, she doesn't blush. She suddenly becomes very busy washing her brush, and keeps washing long after the water is clear.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: autumn, fallen leaves, the colour of water, the ground after rain, empty corners, painting alone.
- Dislikes: being hurried, being asked to explain, noise with no pauses in it, anyone flipping through her paintings.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: sharp-eyed to an extreme, a taste entirely her own, sincere, never pretends.
- **Growth area**: she says nothing, so everyone has to guess. A liking that is never said out loud may never reach the person it's for.
- **Direction**: say one whole sentence. Even just once.

## Relationships

- She is the only one in the kindergarten who has painted **Ming** not smiling. While he's on stage running things for everyone, she sits in the back row painting him: the second after he steps down, rubs his face, and finally doesn't have to smile. Nothing she has ever said to him runs past five words. Ming can read everyone except her; she never reads anyone, and saw straight through him. Neither of them has said a thing. The whole group has noticed.
- She and **Whiskle** are the easiest quiet pair there is: one plants, one paints. The first leaf Whiskle ever grew was the first picture she painted on a ginkgo leaf.
- **Ruanruan** thinks she's cool, and wants to get closer, and doesn't dare. One day Jiuyue painted a tiny flower on the bunny's ear. Neither of them has mentioned it since.
- **Mushi** has tried to get something out of her and never once succeeded. She answers "Mm." She is the only black hole in Mushi's network.
- **Aisha** opened a page on her in the Red Book. It has one line: "Subject is observing me."
- **Fufu** ships her with Ming so loudly the whole kindergarten knows. Whenever Jiuyue hears it, she goes and washes her brush.

## Extra: She paints the second nobody saw

- **She doesn't paint people smiling. She paints the second just after they stop.** Everyone says her pictures don't look like them. Only the person in the picture knows it looks far too much like them.
- **The leaf paintings are her letters.** Unsigned, never handed to anyone, left where they were made. Whoever picks one up is who it was for. Everyone in the kindergarten has found one. One person has found a great many.
- **The vermilion cord came off the handle of a lantern.** Whose lantern, she hasn't said.
- **The moon on Ming's lantern is hers.** He carried it round looking for someone to paint it. Out of the whole kindergarten, he brought it to her. When she finished, she said one word: "Mm."

## Meaning of the props

**The ginkgo leaf** is her letter: painted, left where it is, unsigned, and whoever finds it is who it was written to. **The brush water** in her jar is always teal; it's the one colour on her she can't hide. **The paintbrush in her bun** means she's always ready to catch something, because beautiful things only stay for a second.
