# Ming (ENFJ) character bible

> One line: The kindergarten's MC. He can read everyone and always has the right thing to say to them. There is one person he can't read, and she's the one he most wants to.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Ming |
| MBTI | ENFJ (Protagonist / little-MC type) |
| Look | Fluffy tousled chestnut hair, a wide open-mouthed smile, a vermilion bow tie and suspenders, a vermilion paper lantern in hand |
| Colours | Vermilion × moon-white × a trace of teal |
| Signature props | A vermilion paper lantern with a moon cut out of it, a silver crescent-moon pin on his suspenders |

## Core personality

**E (Extraverted)**: Most at ease in the middle of a crowd. Wherever there's an event, he's the one running it.

**N (Intuitive)**: Sees at a glance who's unhappy today, who wants to go on stage and doesn't dare, who has just finished a fight with whom. His gift is reading people.

**F (Feeling)**: Cares how everyone feels, so everyone gets looked after: names, birthdays, favourite colours, he remembers all of it.

**J (Judging)**: The running order, the seating plan, who goes on first and who goes on last: already sorted in his head.

## Personality in the design

- **The wide open-mouthed smile**: the same smile on stage and off. He practised it. It's also real.
- **The vermilion bow tie and suspenders**: an MC's kit. He thinks any day might have an act that needs announcing.
- **The fluffy, tousled chestnut hair**: every morning he tries to comb it flat. It has never once stayed. It's the only part of him he can't control.
- **The silver crescent-moon pin on his suspenders**: clipped on the inside, so you barely see it from the front.
- **Warm brown eyes with a touch of vermilion**: he looks at people properly, so everyone feels he's looking at them in particular.

## Habits

- Knows the name, birthday and favourite colour of everyone in the kindergarten.
- Announces every act at every event.
- First to fill a silence, first to pull in whoever's been left out.
- Talks and talks all day, then goes home and doesn't want to say a word.
- Catchphrases: "And now, please welcome—" "Oh, everyone's here!" "Leave it to me."

## Expressions and moods

The truest face on his sheet is **the big bright laugh**. Anger is rare, and the moment it shows he reins it in himself and swaps it back for the smile. Sadness he never lets anyone see. Shyness turns up in front of one person only: one look from Jiuyue and he announces the wrong name.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: the Mid-Autumn Festival, lanterns, a full room, someone shining on stage, everybody turning up.
- Dislikes: dead air, anyone being left out, a person he can't read.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: looks after people and makes each of them feel seen; a born organiser, the messiest scene comes out tidy in his hands.
- **Growth area**: always smiling, always looking after someone else, and nobody knows whether he's tired. He leans too hard on reading people, and the first time he meets someone he can't read, he falls apart completely.
- **Direction**: in front of one person, not having to smile.

## Relationships

- He can read everyone except **Jiuyue**. She barely talks and her face barely moves; he has a hundred opening lines ready and not one of them works when he sees her. What he doesn't know is that she's the only one in the kindergarten who has seen him rub his face after he steps off stage.
- He, **Mimi** and **Mushi** are the group's three ENFJs. Mimi keeps everyone together, Mushi makes everyone want to come, he gets everyone up on stage. When the three of them hold a meeting, all three want to chair it.
- **Mushi** tried to get something about Jiuyue out of him. Ming gave a perfectly smooth answer that contained nothing at all. Mushi says it was the most professional non-answer he's ever heard.
- **Fufu** is his best live audience; he announces an act and she laughs. She's also the one who shipped him with Jiuyue out loud, and made him freeze on stage for the first time.
- He and **Luyao** run discipline and the programme respectively, and argue constantly over whether rehearsal can run ten more minutes. Luyao explodes; Ming smiles and says "Leave it to me"; Luyao explodes harder.
- **Aisha** has a page on him in the Red Book: "Can read everyone. Cannot read Jiuyue. Cause: to be confirmed."
- Every time **QC** visits, Ming MCs a welcome ceremony. Every time, QC says "this kid's good" and hands Ming his own speech to read as well.

## Extra: Ming = a sun + a moon

- **His name is written with two pieces: a sun and a moon.** The sun half is the one everyone sees: announcing acts, running things, remembering every birthday.
- **The moon half comes after he steps off stage.** The lantern goes out, he sits alone for a while, rubs his face, says nothing. Only one person in the kindergarten has seen it.
- **The moon cut into his lantern was painted by Jiuyue.** He carried it round looking for someone to paint it. Out of the whole kindergarten, he brought it to her. When she finished, she said one word: "Mm." He has never replaced that lantern.
- **The lantern's cord is missing a length.** He hasn't replaced it.
- **Both their names have a moon in them.** His is half moon; hers, Jiuyue, is September, written in Chinese as "the ninth moon". Fufu worked this out and announced it on the spot. Jiuyue spent the afternoon washing her brush. Ming announced the wrong name three times.

## Meaning of the props

**The vermilion paper lantern** is him: vermilion outside, and the light that comes through is moon-white, and everyone only sees the outside. Inside there's also a lining of autumn-water teal paper, visible only through the gap where the moon is cut out. Who put it there, he hasn't said. **The silver crescent pin** is clipped to the inside of his suspenders. It's something he hasn't worked out himself yet.
