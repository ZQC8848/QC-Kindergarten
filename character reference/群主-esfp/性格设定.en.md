# Qunzhu (ESFP) character bible

> One line: A little shark who wanted to be a person. He got halfway: hands and legs, yes; any taller, no. And with them came the Analects, copied out ten times a day.

## Basics

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Qunzhu |
| MBTI | ESFP (Entertainer / little-show-off type) |
| Look | A lavender shark, a dorsal fin on top of his head, a white belly, tiny eyes set far apart, a grin full of round baby teeth, denim bib overalls, a tail sticking out the back of them |
| Species | Used to be a shark. Now a shark-boy, and the evolution went slightly wrong. |
| Colours | Lavender × white × denim blue |
| Signature props | A chess piece carved with the pawn character, a grid-paper copybook, a pencil covered in tooth marks |

## Core personality

**E (Extraverted)**: Can't be on his own. As a little shark he went looking for Haide every day, and he still does: wherever it's busiest, that's where he squeezes in.

**S (Sensing)**: Only sees what's in front of him. Whatever he wants, he wants now. Taking the long way round, practising slowly: it has never crossed his mind.

**F (Feeling)**: Everything is on his face. Happy, he grins; lose, he cries; jealous, and the whole shark goes sour.

**P (Perceiving)**: No plans. The afternoon the jealousy came up, he was already swimming out to sea to find the witch.

## Personality in the design

- **The lavender shark skin**: sharks are meant to be the scariest thing in the sea. He is lavender.
- **Tiny eyes set far apart**: so far apart they seem to be looking at different things. Say something to him and it takes a moment to land.
- **A row of round baby teeth**: shark teeth, baby edition. The grin looks alarming; the bite doesn't hurt.
- **The tail sticking out of his overalls**: proof the evolution went wrong. The legs came in; the tail never went away.
- **Mitten hands**: the fingers haven't quite separated, so holding a pencil is hard work, which is why he copies the Analects so slowly.
- **Bare feet**: shoes won't go on. The feet are still a bit webbed.

## Habits

- Every day before home time he lies across his desk copying out the Analects ten times. Halfway through he cries, then carries on.
- Loses at chess, cries, then says: "Again!"
- Is always circling Haide. When the girls come over, he puffs out his chest.
- Stops for a long look at every puddle, pond and fish tank.
- Catchphrases: "Again!" "I'm a person now." "…Which time is this?"

## Expressions and moods

The truest face is the one in his poster: head tilted, a row of baby teeth showing, eyes gone blank. Talk to him and that's usually what you get, because he's still thinking. He cries more than anything: crying over chess, crying over copying, crying when Haide is surrounded by girls again. It comes fast and goes fast; wipe his face and the dopey grin is back. Angry, he wants to bite someone, then remembers his teeth are round.

## Likes / dislikes

- Likes: being watched, being praised, playing chess with Haide, water, Mushi.
- Dislikes: losing, being left out, the Analects, writing.

## Strengths and growth

- **Strengths**: warm, direct, sincere. Whoever he likes, he follows around every day; whatever he wants, he says so. He never pretends.
- **Growth area**: he wants to be seen, and wants it in one go. When he envies someone, his first move is a shortcut, not practice. When he loses, all he can do is cry.
- **Direction**: learn one thing slowly, even if it's only winning one game of chess.

## Relationships

- **Haide** is his teacher, his hero and his rival. It was Haide who saw he was clever and taught him chess; it was the girls always around Haide that first taught him what jealousy is. He still hasn't beaten Haide once. Their transformations run in opposite directions: Haide was a boy who became a dog and considers it an upgrade; he was a shark who became a boy and thought that was an upgrade too, and what he got was homework.
- **Mushi** is the one who brought him home from the aquarium, and the one who named him. The day he came storming into the kindergarten as a shark-boy, everyone ran except Mushi, who stood where he was and said: "Wow, you've grown!"
- **The girls** around Haide were his whole reason for going to the witch. The day they really took notice of him was the day the whole kindergarten beat him together.

## Extra: A shark whose evolution went wrong

- **He used to be the smallest shark at the aquarium.** Small, round, and never getting any bigger. The day Mushi and Haide went to the aquarium together, Mushi decided he was far too cute to leave, and took him home.
- **The name.** Mushi called him Qunzhu, "master of the shoal", because a whole school of little fish was always swimming behind him in the tank, and Mushi took him for their leader. In fact the fish were chasing him.
- **He wanted to be impressive.** Haide saw he was clever and taught him chess, but he couldn't do anything, and when he lost all he could do was cry. Over time he grew more and more envious of the girls around Haide.
- **The day the jealousy came up, he swam back out to sea and found a witch.** He wanted to be a person, and he became one, halfway: hands and legs, yes, but still a shark's face, still a tail, and not one inch taller.
- **The first thing he did after evolving was storm the kindergarten.** Then the whole kindergarten beat him together.
- **The price of being a person arrived soon after.** The witch's curse: copy out the Analects ten times a day. In the sea, no shark has ever had homework.

## Meaning of the props

**The pawn** is him: in Chinese chess, once a pawn crosses the river it can never go back, and once he came up out of the sea, neither could he. **The copybook** is what being a person costs, ten times a day. **The tooth marks on the pencil** show how much he hates it: when he can't face another line, he bites.
